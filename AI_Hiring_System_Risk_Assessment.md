
# AI Hiring System - Risk Assessment

## 1. Business Use Case
An organization uses an AI-based tool to screen resumes and shortlist job applicants.

## 2. Identified Risks

| Risk | Description | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Bias | AI may unfairly reject certain groups | High |
| Privacy | Candidate personal data may be exposed | High |
| Accuracy | Qualified candidates may be rejected | High |
| Transparency | Candidates may not understand rejection | Medium |
| Security | Unauthorized access to candidate data | High |

## 3. Risk Mitigation

1. Conduct regular bias assessments.
2. Protect candidate personal information.
3. Keep human reviewers involved.
4. Monitor screening accuracy.
5. Maintain proper audit records.

## 4. Governance Recommendation

The organization should not rely entirely on AI for hiring decisions. Human oversight, transparency and regular risk monitoring should be mandatory.

## 5. Final Conclusion

Responsible AI hiring requires fairness, accountability, privacy protection and human supervision.
