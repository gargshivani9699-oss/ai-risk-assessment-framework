
# AI Risk Assessment Checklist

## 1. AI System Information
- [ ] AI system name identified
- [ ] Business purpose documented
- [ ] Intended users identified
- [ ] Data sources identified

## 2. Risk Identification
- [ ] Data privacy risks reviewed
- [ ] Bias and fairness risks reviewed
- [ ] Accuracy risks reviewed
- [ ] Transparency risks reviewed
- [ ] Security risks reviewed
- [ ] Human oversight requirements reviewed

## 3. Risk Evaluation
- [ ] Likelihood assessed
- [ ] Impact assessed
- [ ] Risk score calculated
- [ ] Risk level classified
- [ ] Critical risks escalated

## 4. Risk Mitigation
- [ ] Appropriate controls identified
- [ ] Human review process established
- [ ] Data protection measures reviewed
- [ ] Bias testing planned
- [ ] Monitoring process defined

## 5. Approval and Monitoring
- [ ] Risk owner assigned
- [ ] Assessment documented
- [ ] Responsible team notified
- [ ] Periodic review scheduled
- [ ] Residual risk evaluated

## Conclusion
This checklist supports a structured and repeatable AI risk assessment process.
