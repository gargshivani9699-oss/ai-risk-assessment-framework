
# AI Risk Assessment Methodology

## 1. Purpose
This methodology defines a structured approach to identifying, evaluating and prioritizing risks associated with AI systems.

## 2. Risk Assessment Parameters

### Likelihood
Measures the probability of a risk occurring.

| Score | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Rare |
| 2 | Unlikely |
| 3 | Possible |
| 4 | Likely |
| 5 | Almost Certain |

### Impact
Measures the potential consequences if a risk occurs.

| Score | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Insignificant |
| 2 | Minor |
| 3 | Moderate |
| 4 | Major |
| 5 | Severe |

## 3. Risk Scoring Formula

Risk Score = Likelihood x Impact

## 4. Risk Classification

| Score | Risk Level |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | Low |
| 5-9 | Medium |
| 10-16 | High |
| 17-25 | Critical |

## 5. Inherent Risk vs Residual Risk

- Inherent Risk: Risk level before applying controls.
- Residual Risk: Risk level remaining after applying controls.

## 6. Risk Treatment

- Avoid: Stop the risky activity.
- Mitigate: Introduce controls to reduce risk.
- Transfer: Share risk through contractual arrangements.
- Accept: Accept the risk with proper authorization.

## 7. Review and Monitoring

AI risks should be reassessed periodically and whenever significant changes occur in the AI system.

## 8. Conclusion

A consistent risk assessment methodology helps organizations prioritize AI risks and implement appropriate governance controls.
