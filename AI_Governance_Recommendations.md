
# AI Governance Recommendations

## 1. Objective
To establish responsible, transparent and accountable AI practices within an organization.

## 2. Key Recommendations

### Fairness
- Conduct regular bias assessments.
- Review AI-generated decisions for unfair outcomes.

### Accountability
- Assign clear responsibility for AI systems.
- Maintain proper audit documentation.

### Transparency
- Document AI system capabilities and limitations.
- Provide appropriate explanations for AI decisions.

### Privacy
- Protect personal and sensitive information.
- Apply data minimization principles.

### Human Oversight
- Maintain human review for high-impact decisions.
- Establish escalation procedures.

## 3. Continuous Monitoring
- Review AI system performance.
- Track incidents and complaints.
- Reassess risks periodically.
- Update controls when necessary.

## 4. Conclusion
Strong AI governance helps organizations ensure responsible AI adoption while managing risks and protecting stakeholders.
