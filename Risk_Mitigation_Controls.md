
# AI Risk Mitigation Controls

## 1. Purpose
This document defines controls to reduce risks associated with AI systems.

## 2. Risk-Based Controls

| Risk | Recommended Control |
|---|---|
| Bias | Regular fairness and bias testing |
| Privacy | Data minimization and access restrictions |
| Accuracy | Output validation and performance monitoring |
| Transparency | Clear documentation and explanations |
| Security | Access control and security assessments |

## 3. Human Oversight
- Human review for high-impact decisions.
- Escalation of critical AI-related incidents.
- Periodic review of AI-generated outcomes.

## 4. Monitoring Controls
- Regular risk assessments.
- Periodic control effectiveness reviews.
- Incident tracking and reporting.
- Documentation of corrective actions.

## 5. Control Ownership

| Control Area | Responsible Team |
|---|---|
| Bias Testing | AI Governance |
| Data Privacy | Privacy Team |
| Accuracy | AI Operations |
| Security | Information Security |
| Transparency | Compliance Team |

## 6. Conclusion
Effective mitigation controls help organizations reduce AI risks and strengthen accountability, fairness and trust.
