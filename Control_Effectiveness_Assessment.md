
# Control Effectiveness Assessment

## 1. Assessment Objective
To evaluate whether implemented AI risk controls are operating effectively.

## 2. Risk Under Assessment
Risk Category: Data Privacy

Risk Description: Unauthorized exposure of candidate personal information.

Inherent Likelihood: 3
Inherent Impact: 5
Inherent Risk Score: 15
Inherent Risk Level: High

## 3. Control Assessment

| Control | Evidence Required | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Data Encryption | Encryption records | Pending |
| Access Control | Access permissions | Pending |
| Data Retention | Retention policy | Pending |
| Activity Monitoring | System logs | Pending |
| Incident Response | Incident records | Pending |

## 4. Assessment Criteria

- Effective: Control is implemented and supported by evidence.
- Partially Effective: Control exists but has implementation gaps.
- Ineffective: Control is missing or not operating properly.
- Not Verified: Insufficient evidence is available.

## 5. Residual Risk Assessment

Residual risk should be evaluated after testing control implementation and effectiveness.

Risk scores must not be reduced merely because controls are documented.

## 6. Recommendations

- Verify encryption implementation.
- Review user access permissions.
- Check data retention practices.
- Review monitoring records.
- Document control weaknesses.

## 7. Conclusion

Control effectiveness assessment helps organizations determine whether AI risk mitigation measures are actually working.
