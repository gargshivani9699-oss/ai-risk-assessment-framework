
# AI Governance Audit Report
## AI-Based Recruitment and Hiring System

### 1. Audit Objective
To assess AI governance risks, control design, evidence availability and risk management practices in an AI-based recruitment system.

### 2. Scope
- AI-based resume screening
- Candidate data privacy
- Bias and fairness
- Candidate ranking accuracy
- Transparency and human oversight
- Employee access management

### 3. Key Risks Identified

Five major risks were identified:
1. Gender bias in candidate shortlisting
2. Candidate personal data exposure
3. Incorrect candidate ranking
4. Lack of transparency
5. Excessive employee access

### 4. Control Assessment

The assessment identified evidence gaps across all five risk areas.

Control effectiveness remains Not Verified because sufficient operating evidence was not available for review.

### 5. Audit Findings

Five findings were documented and corrective actions assigned to relevant control owners.

### 6. Corrective Action Plan

A corrective action plan was developed with:
- Identified gaps
- Corrective actions
- Responsible owners
- Target timelines
- Evidence-based closure criteria

### 7. Risk Assessment Conclusion

The identified risks remain open pending remediation and evidence verification.

Residual risk scores have not been reduced because control effectiveness has not yet been established.

### 8. Final Recommendation

The organization should strengthen:
- Bias testing and monitoring
- Data access governance
- Human oversight
- Transparency mechanisms
- Periodic control reviews

### 9. Audit Status

**Open – Pending Remediation and Evidence Verification**

Note: This is a hypothetical, learning-based case study. No actual corporate audit or independent control testing was performed.
