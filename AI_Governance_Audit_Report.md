
# AI Governance Audit Report
## AI-Based Recruitment and Hiring System

### 1. Audit Objective
To assess AI governance risks, control design, evidence availability and risk management practices in an AI-based recruitment system.

### 2. Scope
- AI-based resume screening
- Candidate data privacy
- Bias and fairness
- Candidate ranking accuracy
- Transparency and human oversight
- Employee access management

### 3. Key Risks Identified

Five major risks were identified:
1. Gender bias in candidate shortlisting
2. Candidate personal data exposure
3. Incorrect candidate ranking
4. Lack of transparency
5. Excessive employee access

### 4. Control Assessment

The assessment identified evidence gaps across all five risk areas.

Control effectiveness remains Not Verified because sufficient operating evidence was not available for review.

### 5. Audit Findings

Five findings were documented and corrective actions assigned to relevant control owners.

### 6. Corrective Action Plan

A corrective action plan was developed with:
- Identified gaps
- Corrective actions
- Responsible owners
- Target timelines
- Evidence-based closure criteria

### 7. Risk Assessment Conclusion

The identified risks remain open pending remediation and evidence verification.

Residual risk scores have not been reduced because control effectiveness has not yet been established.

### 8. Final Recommendation

The organization should strengthen:
- Bias testing and monitoring
- Data access governance
- Human oversight
- Transparency mechanisms
- Periodic control reviews

### 9. Audit Status

**Open – Pending Remediation and Evidence Verification**

Note: This is a hypothetical, learning-based case study. No actual corporate audit or independent control testing was performed.

### Management Responsibilities

Management should:

1. Review significant residual risks.
2. Approve or reject risk acceptance with documented justification.
3. Assign resources and accountable owners.
4. Monitor corrective action progress.
5. Escalate risks that exceed approved risk tolerance.

### Auditor Responsibilities

The auditor should:

1. Report control gaps objectively.
2. Verify remediation evidence.
3. Reassess residual risk.
4. Report unresolved high or critical risks.
5. Maintain an independent assessment.

### Final Principle

Risk acceptance is a management decision, not an auditor's unilateral decision.

## 12. Ongoing AI Governance Monitoring

### 1. Bias and Fairness Monitoring
- Conduct periodic demographic fairness assessments.
- Investigate significant differences in candidate outcomes.
- Maintain testing reports and corrective action records.

### 2. Data Privacy Monitoring
- Review access permissions periodically.
- Monitor access logs for unusual activities.
- Enforce data retention and deletion requirements.

### 3. Human Oversight Monitoring
- Maintain HR review and override records.
- Ensure reviewers can challenge AI recommendations.
- Escalate incorrect or potentially harmful outputs.

### 4. Transparency Monitoring
- Verify candidate notifications.
- Maintain clear information about AI involvement.
- Provide appropriate channels for queries and complaints.

### 5. Access Governance Monitoring
- Review employee permissions.
- Remove unnecessary access.
- Maintain access review evidence.

### Final Recommendation

The organization should establish continuous monitoring, periodic control reviews, documented accountability and evidence-based risk reassessment.
